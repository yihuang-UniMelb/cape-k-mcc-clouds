## Science questions:
(1) Do the open and closed MCC clouds have different aerosol (e.g. CCN/CN) characteristics?
(2) Do the open and closed MCC clouds have different precipitation characteristics?
(3) Are there environmental or meteorological factors (e.g. wind, atmospheric stability) modulate the relationships you observe above?
